❄️ Freezing & storing tips

📦Individual portion containers are best — defrost just what you need
🏷️Label everything: meal name + date. Takes 5 seconds, saves confusion.
🍚Rice: cool it fast (spread it on a tray), fridge within an hour, use within 24 hours, reheat until piping hot. Don't keep it warm on the side.
🍗Cooked chicken keeps up to 3 days in the fridge. Cool it before it goes in, and eat cold or reheat until steaming.
🥔Jacket potatoes: microwave 8–10 mins until soft. Defrosted filling can be reheated in the microwave while the potato cooks.
📅Mince-based dishes and curry keep up to 3 months in the freezer

⭐ Your Favourite Meals — Performance Notes

🍟Loaded fries — chips = fast glycogen replenishment post-CrossFit. Chicken + bacon lardons = solid protein. Add spinach underneath for extra iron with zero effort.
🍝Stuffed pasta + garlic bread — ideal post-run. Carb-rich and fast to make. Choose meat or ricotta stuffed for extra protein.
🫑Stuffed peppers — around 35g protein per serving, great veg, and the filling freezes perfectly. Doubles up as your jacket potato topping.
🥩Egg fried rice + steak — steak is your best iron and zinc source, eggs add more protein. Perfect for the end of a three-session week.
🍗Roasted thighs — more forgiving than breast and cheaper. Roast a full tray and you've got chicken for wraps for days.
🥭Mango curry → crispy salmon bowl — carbs and lean protein one night, omega-3 fats the next. Great recovery pairing.
🌯Enchiladas — chicken and chorizo give a solid protein hit. Cook extra chicken and it becomes lunch wraps.
🍕BBQ chicken pizza — easy carbs on a training day. Add a few slices of leftover chicken on top for extra protein.
🌶️Nando's tray bake — carb-heavy and low effort. Add diced chicken to the tray if you want more protein.

🍫 Pre-Workout Snack Ideas

✅Protein bar — the easy default. Look for one with 15–20g protein you can eat 30–60 mins before.
✅Mini wraps — half a wrap with smoked cheese + salami, or deli chicken + light cream cheese. Keep them small so you're not training on a full stomach.
💡Before a run, go lighter: a bar or half a wrap is plenty.
💡Skipping breakfast? A croissant is mostly fast carbs — it's fine, but the lunch two hours before training does the real fuelling.

🥜 Peanut Butter Alternatives

✅Cream cheese — in a mini wrap or on a bagel. Pairs brilliantly with smoked salmon.
✅Almond butter — if you want something like PB but different. Higher in vitamin E.
✅Avocado — mashed with a pinch of salt. Works on everything.
✅Cottage cheese — as a savoury spread on rice cakes, high protein.

🗓️ Building the Routine

🍟Monday — loaded fries after CrossFit. No cooking decisions, same every week.
🍳Tuesday = batch night. It's a rest day, so you have time: roasted thighs in week 1, stuffed peppers in week 2. Both feed the next couple of lunches.
🍝Wednesday — stuffed pasta after your run. 15 minutes, done.
🥘Weekend — slower cooking: mango curry then crispy salmon bowl in week 1, cheesy potatoes and garlic kiev in week 2.
🥗Salad shortcut — slice cucumber and spring onions for 3 days at once and keep them in a tub. Salad becomes a 2-minute job.

🔄 Fri vs Sat Swapping

✅Completely flexible — tap Friday's badge to make it Rest and Saturday's to make it CrossFit. The pre-workout snack moves with it. Then swap the dinners if you want the bigger meal on the training day.

💪 Protein Across the Day

🎯Aim for roughly 25–35g at each of lunch and dinner, with the rest coming from your pre-workout snack and post-workout milk. Use the tracker on the Meal Plan tab to see where you're at.
⚠️Lighter lunches (nuggets + veg, bagel) are the easiest place to fall short — add protein milk or a bar if you're behind by mid-afternoon.
